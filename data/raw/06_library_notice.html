1. Revised Timings

From 17 August 2026, the central library will operate from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on Monday through Saturday. On Sundays, the library will remain open from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM for reading-room access only. Circulation and book issue services will not be available on Sundays.

2. Borrowing Rules

Undergraduate students may borrow up to three books for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to five books for 21 days. Renewal is permitted once if no other student has placed a reservation on the item.

3. Late Returns

A sample overdue charge of ₹5 per book per day applies after the due date. Students should check the library account before returning books. Damaged or lost books must be reported to library staff.

4. Digital Resources

Students can access subscribed digital resources using their institutional login. Credentials must not be shared. Access may be restricted when subscriptions expire or when a provider is undergoing maintenance.
